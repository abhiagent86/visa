# Visa Q4 & FY2026 earnings brief (27 Oct 2026)

**Headline:** A narrow beat. Q4 non-GAAP EPS was $3.48 against a $3.44 consensus, and revenue was $12.1B against $12.09B. FY26 revenue reached a record $45.9B (+15%), which McInerney called Visa's strongest growth in over 15 years, excluding the post-pandemic rebound and acquisitions. The tone was upbeat, but FY27 guidance points to slower growth, and the shares fell about 2% after hours.

Other items: non-GAAP opex rose 12% in Q4 and 16% for the year. Visa took a $469M litigation provision for the interchange MDL in Q4 ($1.7B for the year), raised the dividend 16% to $0.78, and bought back $20.5B of shares in FY26. The $2.4B BioCatch deal is due to close by the end of Q2 FY27.

## FY2027 outlook
- Net revenue growth: low double digits.
- Non-GAAP EPS growth: low double digits to low teens. That is a step down from FY26's 16%.

## Strategic themes
The release frames the strategy around trust: "trust is the next growth vector for Visa". It backs this with BioCatch (fraud intelligence) and with the "Other" revenue line, which grew 40% and is where much of VAS sits. Call coverage of agentic commerce (Visa Intelligent Commerce, Trusted Agent Protocol), Visa Direct, tokens, stablecoins, Europe/CEMEA and efficiency is not out yet.
