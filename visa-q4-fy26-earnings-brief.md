# Visa Q4 & FY2026 earnings brief (27 Oct 2026, updated with the call)

**Headline:** A narrow beat. Q4 non-GAAP EPS was $3.48 against a $3.44 consensus, and revenue was $12.1B against $12.09B. FY26 brought a record $45.9B in revenue (+15%), and McInerney called it a "milestone year". Value-added services (VAS) and commercial and money movement (CMS) now make up over 40% of net revenue. The FY27 guide is more cautious: revenue growth in the low double digits, a heavier incentive renewal cycle, and Q2 as the low point. The shares fell about 2% after hours.

## FY2027 outlook (adjusted, constant dollars)
- **Net revenue:** low double digits. Q2 is the low point; Q1 is also low double digits.
- **Opex:** low double digits, "modestly below" revenue growth.
- **EPS:** low double digits to low teens; Q1 at the "low end of mid-teens".
- **VAS:** growth in the low-to-mid 20s.
- **Renewals:** about 25% of payments volume renews in FY27, against 20% in FY26, so incentives will be higher.

## Strategic themes
- **Agentic commerce:** still early and small in FY27. Nearly 50 AI partners are live. Visa is working on Visa Intelligent Commerce, an agentic token, the Trusted Agent Protocol and an agent directory, and is working with Anthropic on commerce blueprints.
- **Tokens:** over 50% of credentials and over 60% of e-commerce transactions are tokenised.
- **Visa Direct:** transactions up 23% in Q4; over 15B transactions for the year.
- **Stablecoins:** settlement run rate above $25B, up 10x, across 10 blockchains.
- **AI internally:** over 60% of product and engineering teams use agentic tooling (target: 100%), and product releases rose 25%.
- **CEMEA:** travel cross-border volume rose 13% and CEMEA improved, but the Middle East conflict is a near-term risk. Pismo signed clients in Sweden and Belgium.
- **Regulation:** Visa took a $469M provision for the US interchange case and funded $405M into its litigation escrow. BioCatch ($2.4B) is due to close by Q2 FY27, and China domestic-processing preparation has been approved.

## Interview angles
1. **Deal economics:** "A quarter of payments volume renews in FY27 while incentives already grow faster than revenue. A rigorous deal desk and incentive-ROI tracking is where Sales Ops earns its keep."
2. **Growing existing clients:** "VAS is $14B growing 30%, and VAS plus CMS is over 40% of revenue. I'd build CEMEA coverage, pipeline and targets around VAS attach and growth from existing clients, not just volume."
3. **AI operating leverage:** "Visa is driving agentic tools across engineering. I'd bring the same discipline to sales — forecasting, quote-to-cash, account planning — so opex grows more slowly than revenue and sellers have time for new motions like agentic commerce."
